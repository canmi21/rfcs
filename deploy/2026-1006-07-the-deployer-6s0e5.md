## Question

host deploys an image a run builds, and the Workers were deployed by Cloudflare's Git integration,
apart from every record the platform keeps of a deploy. Who deploys a Worker?

## Evidence

platform's `spec/architecture/deployer.md`. The Workers still to move onto it are RFC 4s460.

## Decision

- **The deployer**, `apps/system/deployer` on `tyo`, deploys each Worker artifact a run builds, as
  host deploys an image.
- **The hook tells it of every run** as its third receiver, through the nodes it is placed on.
- **web is admitted**: its `deploy.yml`, its webhook and its runs passed on to the deployer.
- **A Worker nobody owns is recorded as skipped**, and a failed deploy keeps wrangler's output,
  redacted.
