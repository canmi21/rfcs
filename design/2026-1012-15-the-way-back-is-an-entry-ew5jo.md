## Question

Inside a level, the console's sidebar and space's head their pages with the way back up: a chevron
and the name of the list it was entered from, laid out as a page with no ground of its own, only its
color answering the pointer. With the keyboard's ring drawn flush on a control's shape and on bare
words' ink, what is the way back, and what mark does it carry?

## Options

- Keep it groundless, ringed on its words as bare words are.
- Make it an entry as any other, ground and ring and all, with a back arrow in the icon column.
- Give the list it leads to an icon of its own, and set a back mark before it.

## Evidence

Groundless, the way back took the kit's ring on its padded box, a shape it never shows, so the ring
read as neither a control's nor words'. Its chevron, thinner and lighter than the entries' icons in
the same column, read as an indent rather than a mark. A second icon for the list it leads to would
stand two marks in a row whose column holds one, and the list is often a layer, which has no icon and
is never a page.

## Decision

- **The way back is an entry as any other**: its size, its icon column, its type, the pointer's tint
  over it, and the keyboard's ring flush on its row.
- **Its mark is a back arrow**, `arrow-left`, at the entries' size and stroke, in their column.
- **It is never the page open**, so it is never held selected.
- In the console's sidebar and in space's alike.
