## Question

A picture is served at `pictures/<id>.png`, to whoever holds the id, for as long as it is kept --
platform's `spec/architecture/shot.md`. What it lacks is a place that issues a temporary file -- an
address signed for a while and then refused -- which the route would give way to. Until one exists
the route stays as it is. Where that place is, and whose it is, is open.
