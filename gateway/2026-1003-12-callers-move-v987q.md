## Question

With the new domains answering, every caller still named the old hosts and the unversioned paths.
E8 of the gateway's milestones.

## Decision

- **The URL library names the new hosts, and every caller here follows**, GitHub's webhook with
  them; each retired host is pinned at the version its old paths were spelled for.
- **Our own callers move like everyone else's**, calling the public hostnames through the Tunnel and
  back, a round trip and nothing else.
- **The unversioned paths of E4 go** with their last caller.
